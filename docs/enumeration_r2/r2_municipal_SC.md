### Gap diff findings against the engine
The existing `src/foreclosure_scraper/scrapers/city_websites/cities.py` has two live defects:
1. **`QUERIES[:3]` slice** — only `foreclosure`, `tax sale`, `delinquent tax` ever run. `code enforcement`, `nuisance abatement`, `demolition` are defined but **never executed**, which is precisely this target class.
2. **Six dead domains** in `CITIES` return 000 and silently yield nothing: `cityofinmansc.org` → `cityofinman.org`; `cityoflibertysc.com` → `libertysc.com`; `cityofunionsc.org` → `cityofunion.net`; `cityofwestminstersc.com` → `westminstersc.org`; `townofblacksburg.com` → `townofblacksburgsc.com`; `cityofgaffneysc.gov` → `cityofgaffney-sc.gov`. Also **Greer is absent entirely** despite being the largest municipality in the footprint after Spartanburg/Anderson, and its domain moved to `cityofgreersc.gov`.

### Privacy note
`gis.cityofandersonsc.com/arcgis/rest/services/EmergencyServices/ProbationList` (FeatureServer + MapServer) is publicly reachable and appears to be an individual-level criminal-justice roster. I did **not** query it, enumerate its fields, or retrieve any record. Flagging it as an exposure to report, not a source to work.

### Top 5 net-new
1. **Spartanburg city Master Condemnation List** — 94 properties with owner + mailing address + parcel PIN + condemnation date, already resolved. Bypasses the name→parcel ~25–30% ceiling and the `owner_mailing` spine entirely; 37 are 2025–26 and a large share are absentee/out-of-state. Highest signal-per-row found anywhere in this sweep.
2. **Anderson city ViewPointCloud City Parcels** — 13,472 parcels with **recorded sale price** plus 13,846 with owner mailing address, free and no-login. Directly answers the "SC recorded-$/sqft comps paywalled" and "deed-OCR non-viable" walls for the Anderson city footprint.
3. **Spartanburg Tax Sale Parcels (163)** — carries a CAMA **condition** field ("POOR") alongside owner mailing and tax owed. Condition is the input the vision/CAMA-condition enrichers are trying to infer from photos; here it is stated.
4. **Clinton city ArcGIS server** — an entire previously-unknown municipal server: 3,828 parcels with owner+mailing+deed refs, 70 city-owned surplus parcels, and 13,210 address points carrying WATER/SEWER/POWER utility fields (a genuine occupancy/vacancy proxy that does not depend on USPS).
5. **Spartanburg Infill Eligible (1,076) + City-Owned/Unused (433/343/116)** — the city's own curated buildable/disposable inventory with the full owner spine; a ready-made dispo and buy-box overlay that sits on an org the engine already touches but only reads one layer of.
