### Re-test verdict on the "no ArcGIS endpoint" claim

- **Union SC — prior conclusion HOLDS.** Hard-tested: `/arcgis/rest/*`, `/server/rest/*`, `/tgis/arcgis/rest/*` on `unionsc.wthgis.com` all 404; no county portal; no AGOL org. The three AGOL hits for "Union County South Carolina" were **false positives — University of South Carolina** (orgs `uscstaff`, `southcarolina`). County GIS is qPublic + WTH TGIS (non-Esri).
- **Cherokee SC — prior conclusion PARTIALLY WRONG.** The *county* has no ArcGIS (qPublic only), but the **consultant-hosted `canoewood` org for the City of Gaffney** publishes Cherokee-area parcels (12,764) and address points (7,116) via AGOL. That is exactly the "GIS vendor hosting the county's data under its own domain" case the Anderson lesson predicted. Separately, `jweaver_cherokee911` (org `6Y1Df7jaLg0NtBFj`) is **Cherokee County ALABAMA**, not SC — its layers reference Little River Canyon, DeKalb/Etowah/Chattooga counties. Do not ingest it.

### Method result

The portal-index-vs-REST-directory diff yielded hidden services in **2 of 7** counties: Anderson (15, already documented) and Spartanburg (16, net-new). Pickens, Oconee, Laurens, Gaffney/Cherokee all showed **exact parity** — their REST directories are not filtered. The higher-value discovery pattern in SC turned out to be **undisclosed sibling web adaptors** (Spartanburg `/image`) and **undisclosed server hostnames** (Anderson `propertyviewer`), both of which only the portal item index reveals.
