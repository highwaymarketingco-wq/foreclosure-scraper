### Top 3 net-new

1. **`Posting3` + `dqnt_2024` + `DelParces_October2025NewsAd` — the annual delinquent-tax list as a live, queryable feature service.** Pickens republishes its tax-sale list to a fresh AGOL service every year and links it from the Delinquent Tax FAQ ("The map of the properties will be here"). The engine currently only parses the *post-sale* results PDF (`pickens_tax_sale`, 160 rows) — it is missing the **pre-sale list, which is where the actionable equity is**. `dqnt_2024` alone is 954 rows carrying **owner name + owner mailing address + situs address + amount due**, i.e. a complete mail spine with no name→parcel resolution step and no SCDOT dependency. Across years: 311 + 954 + 362 + 834 + 397 + 436 + 412 + 362 rows, all `f=json`, no auth.

2. **`FLC_2022` — the Forfeited Land Commission inventory, with sale prices.** 18 tracts the county bid in at tax sale and still holds (PIN, OWNER, SALE_PRICE, YEAR 2017–2022). SC's FLC properties are buy-direct-from-county, no auction, no redemption competition. `source_history.json` has `sc_flc`, `horry_flc`, `spartanburg_flc`, `terry_howe_flc` — **no Pickens FLC source exists**. Pair with `vacant_co_prop` (14) and `county_prop` (76) for the full county-held disposal pool.

3. **`Pickens_Open_data/FeatureServer/6` — 66,417 parcels with owner mailing address, free and unwalled.** Memory records SCDOT `SC_Parcels` as now token-walled (silent 200+error) and the name→parcel ceiling at ~25–30%. This layer sidesteps that entirely for Pickens: PIN + NAME1/NAME2 + ADD1/CITY/STATE/ZIP + LOCADD + ACRES + BLDGS + SALEDT/SALEP + ZONINGCODE, plus `Energov_AGOL/FeatureServer/7` adds **ACTUALVAL** and `Pickens_Open_data/FeatureServer/0` adds 66,166 geocoded address points with Lat/Long. That is a countywide parcel + valuation + situs spine for one county, obtained from three unauthenticated GETs.
