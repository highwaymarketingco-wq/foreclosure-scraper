## Top 3 net-new

1. **Catalis unpaid-tax-bill API** — `POST https://d1ebsyxxbc7tep.cloudfront.net/data/2e2e0f49-98ab-4085-b874-0143aacc62ac/Records`. Unauthenticated JSON, no CAPTCHA, returning owner name + **mailing address** + situs + parcel + **dollar amount owed** + due date, filterable by `payStatus:"Unpaid"` across years 1993–2026. This closes the "taxes-owed not captured" gap for McDowell outright, and does it year-by-year (2024 Unpaid alone already pages out at the 1,000-row cap). Enumerable by iterating `parameter:"Owner Name"` over letter prefixes or `"Map Number"` over parcel prefixes.

2. **`www.webgis.net/arcgis/rest/services/NC/McDowell/MapServer` layers 2 and 4** — the county's own parcel fabric, which the engine currently misses because it uses the NC OneMap republish. Layer 2 is 653 parcels larger and carries `TotalAssessedValue` + deed book/page; layer 4 adds **three generations of sale history** plus `GIS_Lat`/`GIS_Long` (a native lat/long that sidesteps the geocode-at-1/sec bottleneck) and `OGovMATID` joining parcels to the permit system. Both paginate.

3. **SmartGov permit portal at `co-mcdowell-nc.smartgovcommunity.com`** — an act-on-it layer McDowell has and the engine does not touch: four permit reports served without login, plus a Helene "Flooded Structures and Compromised Electrical Systems" document set. The `OGovMATID` field in GIS layer 4 is the join key back to parcels, which makes permit-derived distress (open/expired/no-permit-after-damage) resolvable to owner and mailing address.
