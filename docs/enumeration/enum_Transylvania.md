## Top 3 net-new

1. **`Damage_Assessment_Viewer` + `Wildfire Cleanout_results` on AGOL org `ProOLvsmwpY1RmFG`.** 147 damage rows (143 with PIN, severity, and repair `cost`) plus 113 wildfire-assessment rows carrying **homeowner name, phone, occupancy, and disability flags**. Both are open ArcGIS queries, both join straight to the parcel layer by PIN/address, and `enrichment_helene_damage.py` does not know this org exists. This is the single highest-density distress signal in the county.

2. **`https://gis.transylvaniacounty.org/GISReports/propertyreports/Parcel_{PIN}-{CARD}.pdf`.** The engine skipped Transylvania appraisal cards because `enrichment_assessor_photo.py:47` records "`CARD` + `Report_URL` — both 100% NULL". `Report_URL` is indeed 0/31,751, but **`CARD` is populated on 31,633 rows**, and the map's Arcade `expr5` shows the URL is built from `PIN + '-' + CARD`. I fetched two PINs cold: 632 KB and 607 KB PDFs. That is a 31.6k-parcel document lane currently switched off by a wrong assumption — a one-line fix.

3. **`CAD_Calls_For_Service_Closed_view` — 305,856 geocoded closed 911/CAD calls.** `fulladdr` + `calltype` + `calldate` + `disposition`, 95,101 of them since Jan 2025, with directly usable slices (CIVIL PROCESS 4,147; STRUCTURE FIRE 247; OVERDOSE 235; DEATH 103). No other county in the footprint has been found publishing a full closed-call history at this granularity, and it turns address-level life-event distress into a queryable feed.

Runner-up worth flagging: `nc_govdeals_real_property.py:136` hard-codes `"Transylvania": None` with the comment *"legacy storefront tcncre — non-resolving"*, yet the county's **February 2026** legal ad still directs bidders to `https://www.GovDeals.com/tcncre` for a live surplus-real-property auction that ran 3/23–4/22/2026. That storefront should be re-enabled, not left disabled.
