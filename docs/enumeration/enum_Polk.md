## Top 3 net-new

1. **`LU_Parcels` — 980 present-use-value parcels with full owner + mailing.** This is a genuine distress *class* the engine has no concept of anywhere: NC present-use value defers taxes, and disqualification or transfer triggers three years of rollback taxes plus interest. Verified sample shows a 1,530-acre tract carrying a $4,033 levy against an $802,894 tax value. Owner name, mailing address, acreage and deed book/page are all in the same response, so it drops straight onto the name→property→equity→contact backbone with no resolver work.

2. **`Public_Lands_Features` — 149 government/institutional parcels, full CAMA schema.** Polk publishes no surplus-property page at all (the sweep confirmed `public_notices.php` and `planning.php` are 404), so this layer is the only machine-readable view of county-held inventory, including parcels acquired through prior tax foreclosures. Pair it with the CivicClerk agenda portal to catch disposition resolutions before they are advertised.

3. **`ParcelNumber` + `Site_Addresses` as the situs-resolution pair.** `ParcelNumber` carries 17,549 rows — 144 more than the `Parcels` layer the engine already reads — and `Site_Addresses` gives 20,760 E911 points with `Long`/`Lat`, `BLDG_TYPE` and `STRUCTURE_DESCRIPTION`. Neither is referenced anywhere in `src/`. Given the known name→parcel ceiling of roughly 25-30%, this is the cheapest available lift for Polk situs coverage. The vacancy angle is honestly thin, though: `MISC_TYPE='OLD ADDRESS'` yields only 89 records and `U/C` only 11, and there is no condemnation or nuisance flag in any Polk layer.

One correction worth flagging against likely prior assumptions: **`TOTAL_TAX_OWED` is not a delinquent balance.** It is populated on 17,153 of 17,405 parcels and tracks assessed value at roughly 0.52%, with Polk County itself and the Board of Education among the top values — it is the annual levy. Any lead scoring that reads that field as arrears will misfire across the entire county.
