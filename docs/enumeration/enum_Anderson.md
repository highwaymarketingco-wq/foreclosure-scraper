### Sweep-by-sweep results

- **Sweep 1 (ArcGIS Server):** `gis.andersoncountysc.org` and `maps.andersoncountysc.org` do not resolve. Two real roots found and **fully recursed**: `propertyviewer.andersoncountysc.org/arcgis` (11.3 — 6 folders, 18 root services) and `gis.cityofandersonsc.com/arcgis` (10.61 — 14 folders). Every folder enumerated; every distress-hinting layer probed for count + fields. Highest yield of any sweep after ACPASS.
- **Sweep 2 (ArcGIS Online):** **Nothing.** No county AGOL org exists. `owner:andersoncountysc` = 0 results; `"Anderson County SC foreclosure"` = 0; `"…tax sale"` = 0; `andersoncountysc.org` = 0. The generic `anderson county south carolina` query returned 51 items, all Virginia Tech/Clemson student coursework, redlining layers, or other counties. `owner:cityofandersonsc` = 2 items (Zoning, DOWNTOWN). No Web Experience / definitionExpression curated list of the Henderson kind exists here — the county runs on-prem Enterprise, and its `/arcgis/sharing/rest/portals/self` returns an ArcGIS Web Adaptor error page. **The Henderson-equivalent hiding place in Anderson is the ACPASS CGI layer, not AGOL.**
- **Sweep 3 (site structure):** `sitemap.xml` empty; `sitemap_index.xml` → 10 child sitemaps. `page-sitemap.xml` surfaced assessor, clerk-of-court, **master-in-equity**, probate, register-of-deeds, treasurer, magistrates, environmental-code-enforcement, building-codes. robots.txt is stock WordPress (no useful disallows).
- **Sweep 4 (search):** Produced the Master-in-Equity PDF path pattern (`/wp-content/uploads/YYYY/MM/<Month>-D-YYYY-Sale-List.pdf`) and the Forfeited Land Commission angle. Confirmed no free bulk parcel shapefile (ReportAll/Dynamo/Regrid are paid). No new hosts beyond those already found.
- **Sweep 5 (vendors):** Tenant = **PostingPro/Palmetto Posting** (tax sale), **Zuercher** (jail), **GovDeals** agency 8531, **Municode**, **qPayBill** (`andersonwastewater.qpaybill.com` — wastewater utility only, *not* property tax, unlike the Spartanburg qPayBill win). Not a tenant: qPublic/Schneider, DEVNET Wedge, Kofile publicsearch.us, Cott, Logan deeds, AcclaimWeb, policetocitizen, webtaxpay, bttaxpayerportal, Catalis.
- **Sweep 6 (raw HTML):** The Tax Sale page's raw HTML is what exposed `anderson.postingpro.net` and GovDeals `agency=8531` — neither is visible as link text. The Master-in-Equity page's HTML holds **217 PDF hrefs** vs. a handful visible. `propertyviewer.../mapsjs/` loads `assets/Anderson/{LayerConfig,SearchConfig,config}.js`; I pulled all three and cross-checked — they reference only services I had already enumerated, so the viewer holds no hidden layer.

### Top 3 net-new

1. **`asrdetail1.cgi` (ACPASS parcel record).** One keyless GET returns owner **mailing address**, previous owner, full **priced** sale history, market/tax value and assessment history. This is the whole name→property→equity→contact backbone for Anderson in a single endpoint, and it directly addresses the `owner_mailing` mail-spine breakage noted in `project_resolver_bugs_and_scdot_wall`. Reachable by name via `asrmain.cgi` or by TMS from the 120k-row ArcGIS parcel layer.
2. **`rpcmapnbrb.cgi` → `rpcdetail1.cgi` (tax balance).** Per-parcel, per-year **Balance Due**, paid date, penalty/interest, and an **Escrow Act** lender flag. `project_enrichment_pipeline_facts` lists taxes-owed as an explicit uncaptured gap; this closes it for Anderson without the qPayBill/TMS-join trick that only worked in Spartanburg.
3. **Master-in-Equity sale list + results archive (217 PDFs).** Not just the upcoming roster but ~6 years of *outcomes*, with deficiency, withdrawal, bankruptcy and redemption flags, defendant names including estate PRs, and street addresses — a clean training/backtest corpus plus a live monthly feed at a predictable URL.

**Also worth flagging:** `salmaine.cgi` + `saldetail1.cgi` yields free recorded **sale prices** month-by-month (824 in June 2026 alone). Memory note `project_sc_deed_ocr_yield` concluded SC sale prices were not obtainable free; that holds for ROD deed images, but is **false for Anderson via ACPASS** — worth correcting before more OCR effort is spent there.
