### Top 3 net-new

1. **`TR-452 Delinquent Bills Report w Parcel Id.xlsx`** — one unauthenticated 1.3 MB download at `https://www.rutherfordcountync.gov/TR-452%20Delinquent%20Bills%20Report%20w%20Parcel%20Id.xlsx` gives **~9,337 TY2025 delinquent real-estate bills totalling $5.64 M**, each with taxpayer name, situs/legal description, **parcel id**, and **amount due**. Zero pagination, zero scraping. Reachable only from the raw HTML of the tax index page (the anchor sits under an unrelated MyDMV block). This directly fills the repo's long-standing "taxes-owed not captured" gap for Rutherford.

2. **Sturgis Avalon `Wildfire/Records` bulk enumeration** — posting `{"value":"","facets":{"Status":{"Unpaid":true}}}` to `https://d1ebsyxxbc7tep.cloudfront.net/data/5b88e44b-0038-4361-8c53-7ce1343ad3ad/Wildfire/Records` returns **29,319 genuinely delinquent bills (TY2016–2025)** out of 95,902 unpaid, with owner name, **full mailing address**, situs, parcel number, amount due, and status flags including **`ADVERTISED`** (tax-lien advertisement list) and **`OUTSIDE LAW FIRM`** (already handed to foreclosure counsel — the highest-intent slice in the county). Pages 20/request via `skip` + the `SearchToken` header echoed from page 1. `docs/completeness_taxNC.md` records this source as "per-parcel only" — that is a **false wall**, now disproven.

3. **`EnerGov/EnerGov/FeatureServer/3`** (or its twin `TemplateSite/SearchableLayers/MapServer/1`) — a genuinely paginated FeatureServer carrying all **57,767 parcels with owner, full mailing address, physical/situs address, assessed values, deed book/page/date, sale price, heated area, `Use_Value_Deferred`, `Under_Assmt_Flag`, and `Exemption_Description`**. It resolves the explicit TODO left in `enrichment_arcgis.py:92` and yields **8,728 out-of-state absentee owners** and **2,179 PUV-deferred parcels** as standing filters, without a single name lookup.
