### Top 3 net-new

1. **`DT2025` / `DT2024` / `DT2023` FeatureServers** — 1,561 delinquent-tax parcels across three years, every row carrying **owner name, TMS, dollars owed, and lat/lng**, $792,871.94 outstanding in 2025 alone. Zero references to `DT2025|DT2024|DT2023` anywhere in `src/`. This is the *delinquent roll*, structurally different from `counties_sc.oconee_tax_sale` (which reads the annual **sale** sheet and is empty until Oct 21, 2026). It also directly closes the "taxes-owed NOT captured" gap for this county, and the 3-year stack yields a repeat-delinquency score no other Oconee source gives.

2. **`Assignment_FLC/FeatureServer/1`** — the county runs **two** FLC services. The engine reads `Assignment_Availability` (76 FLC rows); the un-referenced `Assignment_FLC` holds **468 rows, 434 flagged `Status='NONE'` = still buyable**, with the full money trail (`Bid, DT_Cost, PriorYr_Tax, Current_Tax, Total_Tax, Abated_Tax, Proc_Fee, FLC_Price, Sale_Date`). Same "hidden second service" shape as the Henderson miss.

3. **`CitizenServe/MapServer/4`&`/5` — `GISDATA.dbo.assessordata`** — a 68,145-row assessor table hanging off a *permits* map service, exposing `current_owner + owner_street + owner_citystate + owner_zip + deed_book/page + legal_descr` for the entire county, bulk-pageable at 1,000/request with no key. The engine currently hits `PARCELDATA_owner_Assr` per parcel; this is the whole mail spine in ~69 requests, and it is the single best lever against the ~25-30% name→parcel ceiling for Oconee.

**Runner-up worth noting:** `ReinvestmentZone/FeatureServer/0` is the only free Oconee layer found that carries **assessed and total market value** (`ROUNDED_AS`, `TOTAL_MARK`) — small (379 parcels) but it breaks the "SC free GIS parcel layers are cadastral-only" assumption recorded in `sc_assessor_cama.py`.
