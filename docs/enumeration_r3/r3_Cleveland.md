**Biggest remaining gap:** taxes owed. Cleveland publishes **no** online bill balance or delinquent search anywhere — `clevelandcountytaxes.com` is a Catalis SPA whose only sections are `/#property`, `/#vehicles`, `/#estimator` (`/pages/search`, `/pages/taxbills` both 404), there is no NCPTS tenancy and no per-year unpaid service on either GIS host — so the annual G.S. 105-369 lien advertisement on `ncnotices.com` remains the single route to a delinquent list, and every free owner-mailing address in the county is truncated to 20 characters at the CAMA extract.
