**Top 5 net-new**

1. **Beacon sales query (`btnRecentSalesByParcelIDGroup`)** — a date-ranged, map-sheet-scoped sales feed with **Sale Price, Sale Qual, Book/Page, Class, Acres, Sq Ft**. This is a free countywide comp spine in a state where recorded-consideration comps were written off as paywalled. ~165 map sheets and it is complete.
2. **Beacon owner-name search as a distress classifier** — substring matching over the full owner string turns the assessor roll into a cohort query: ≥500 `DEC'D`, 422 `TRUST`, 73 `ET AL`, plus `(LE)` life estates. Heir/probate leads without touching the walled court index.
3. **Zuercher `union-so-sc`** — 113 in custody with **full DOB on 113/113**, and round 1's "not a tenant" was simply the wrong slug. It comes with a hard caveat: `robots.txt` is `Disallow: /` on Union **and on the already-wired Cherokee and Anderson tenants**, so this is a policy wall to log, not a source to wire.
4. **WTH tgis dsid 992 `ParcelJoin.tml`** — ~18–19k sequentially indexed ParcelIDs, the exact layer round 1 missed by assuming the parcel data stopped at dsid 988. It hands you the complete keyspace that drives both Beacon and qPayBill.
5. **qPayBill map-sheet enumeration** — `ddlCriteriaList=Map` + a 3-digit prefix returns an entire map sheet of tax records with **dollar amounts and TMS**, upgrading a per-parcel lookup into a bulk delinquent-roll pull.

**Biggest remaining gap:** everything court-shaped — foreclosure/lis pendens, probate estates, judgments, divorce — is behind the SC Public Index WAF with no Master-in-Equity roster, no county legal-notice feed, and no probate index anywhere online, so Union's entire litigation-driven distress signal is manual-export-only.
