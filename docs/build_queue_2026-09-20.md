# Build queue, 2026-09-20

A verified, de-duplicated list of what is left to build for the 18-county footprint. Every item was checked against `src/foreclosure_scraper` and `scripts/` before it was listed as open. Where a doc says something is open and the repo says it is built, the doc claim is in the STALE table (section D). Where the repo shows the wall, the item is in the WALLED table (section C) and not in the queue.

Layers are the 2026-09-20 ledger definitions. **Lift** is expected percentage points on the named layer, averaged over the listed counties; an empty signal cell counts as 10 points times the probability stated. **Score** is lift x counties / engineer-hours. Sections: A top 10, B full queue by layer, C walls, D stale claims, E stored-but-unscored, F owner decisions, G order of work, H method and caveats.

**Two findings change the plan.** (1) The live Anderson county layer no longer publishes owner names or mailing addresses, so the 2026-08-03 offline roll `data/sc_parcel_mailing.db` is now irreplaceable (X-01) and is also the fastest Anderson identity fix (I-01). (2) The cheapest large lifts are wiring defects, not new sources: real Transylvania mailing and balances and the SC qPayBill balances are collected and never read (M-01, D-01).

**Queue size:** 67 items. By class: BUILD-READY 46, BUILD-WITH-WORK 17, OPERATOR-MANUAL 4. The WALLED table in section C has 15 walls and 6 posture conflicts; the STALE table has 33 rows.

- **Sources mined:** `docs/path_to_100.md`, `path_to_100_deepdive.md`, `gap_closure_plan_2026-09-06.md`, `extraction_gaps.md`, `net_new_source_register.md`, `gap_ledger.md`, `coverage_gaps.md`, `dirty_deeds_synthesis_2026-09-10.md`, `source_reverification_2026-09-10.md`, `manual_source_inventory.md`, `blocked_sources_forensic.md`, `COUNTY_SYSTEMS_REGISTRY.md`, plus `MASTER_GAPS_WALLS_AND_MANUAL_LANES.md` (hard rules) and, because it is the live worklog, `WEEKEND_LOOP_QUEUE.md`. `docs/net_new_source_register.md` is truncated at the head in the repo (sections 1.1 to 1.14 are missing), so the NC counties, Spartanburg, Anderson and most of Pickens were covered through its top-20 list, its wall section and the repo.
- **What was and was not touched:** the board (`docs/listings*.json.gz`, `load_board`, `iter_board_rows`) was never loaded and no repo script was run. Verification is grep, file reads, git history, read-only SQLite counts on `data/parcel_cache/*.sqlite` and `data/sc_parcel_mailing.db`, and about 30 read-only requests to public ArcGIS endpoints on 2026-09-20 (field lists, counts and folder listings for Anderson `County_Parcels`, Anderson layer 6 and the city catalog, Union AGOL, the Pickens EnerGov mirror, the Oconee, Laurens and Cherokee address points) plus one HEAD request on the Spartanburg extract. Nothing was downloaded. No file other than this one was edited.
- **Lift** is the expected gain in percentage points of the named layer's share, averaged over the counties listed, using the 2026-09-20 ledger definitions (`scripts/coverage_100_ledger.py`): identity is parcel id plus situs; value is any market, appraised or tax value; mail needs an owner name plus a mailing; actual debt is `raw['tax_owed'].balance` or an `amount_owed` marked actual. For an empty signal cell the convention is 10 points per cell filled, multiplied by the probability stated in the basis. Every lift states its basis; where the basis is a guess the confidence column says Low.
- **Score** is lift x number of counties / engineer-hours, exactly as requested. Items that do not move a ledger layer (sqft, ARV, lead breadth, outreach gates) are listed but not ranked, because a score would compare unlike things.
- **County row counts** are cited only where a doc or `docs/run_meta.json` `by_county_top` gives them (2026-09-20: Spartanburg 6,195; Buncombe 5,490; Rutherford 4,455; Pickens 2,711; Henderson 1,786; McDowell 1,582; Oconee 1,315; Anderson 1,230; Cherokee 1,136; Laurens 979; Gaston 900; Lincoln 669; Union 496; the other five counties are under 500 each). Those figures are a top-15 list of unclear basis and do not add to the 75K footprint rows in the brief, so treat them as approximate.
- **Classes:** BUILD-READY (free, no wall, endpoint known), BUILD-WITH-WORK (needs recon or a real parser), WALLED, OPERATOR-MANUAL. Several items are marked BUILD-READY only after a 30-minute live check of a field name; the Depends column says so.
- **Depth of verification:** evidence lines for items outside the top 10 came from five parallel read-only reviewers who grepped the repo and read git history. I re-verified by hand every top-10 item, the Anderson findings (owner roll, live schema, zero callers), the Transylvania and qPayBill balance and mailing gaps, and the divorce, jail and incarceration items. Line numbers can drift by a few lines between reviewers and files.
- **Not measured:** no lift here is a measured board delta. The board was not loaded, so every lift is derived from a doc figure, a repo comment, a local SQLite count or a live layer count, and each row says which. Run M-03 (dry-run) and the ledger script before and after any item to replace the estimate with a number.
